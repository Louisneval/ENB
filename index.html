Privacy Policy

This is a private personal application used only by its owner. It is not offered to or used by any other person.

The application reads account balances and transactions from the owner's own bank accounts via Enable Banking (read-only). All data is stored encrypted on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or sent to any server other than Enable Banking for the purpose of retrieving the owner's own account information.

Contact for data protection matters: the owner, using the contact details given to Enable Banking at registration.
